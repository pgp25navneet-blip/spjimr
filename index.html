Frequently Asked Questions

Can I bring my family or spouse?

Yes! Spouses and kids are welcome to join the event. Please specify the number of guests in the RSVP form so we can plan catering accordingly.

Is accommodation provided on campus?

Limited executive rooms on campus are available on a first-come, first-served basis. Partner hotel discounts nearby will also be shared in your confirmation email.

What is the dress code for the event?

Daytime sessions are Smart Casuals. The Gala Dinner on Saturday evening is Formal / Festive Ethnic Wear.
